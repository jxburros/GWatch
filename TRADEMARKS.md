You are welcome to build a paid business around installing, hosting,
supporting, or customizing GWatch for customers (see `LICENSE` Section 2).
You may factually describe that business as offering "GWatch deployment
and support" or "GWatch customization services" — but the required
non-official notice still applies, and your business name and primary
branding should be your own, not "GWatch" itself, to avoid implying you
are JX Holdings or an authorized reseller.
