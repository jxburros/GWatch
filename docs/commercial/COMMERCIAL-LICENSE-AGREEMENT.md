## Background

GWatch is published by JX Holdings under the GWatch Community License 1.0 (the
"**Community License**"), which already lets anyone use, modify and host GWatch
free of charge. Customer wants a signed agreement, defined support and the
other benefits described below. This Agreement provides them, and sits on top
of the Community License without taking any of its rights away.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Software"** means the GWatch software published by JX Holdings, including
  the GWatch hardware agent and MCP companion, in source and object form, and
  any Release of it provided during the Term.
- **"Release"** means a version of the Software that JX Holdings publishes as a
  signed release at <https://github.com/jxburros/GWatch/releases>, including a
  patch build JX Holdings gives Customer directly under the Support Plan.
- **"Documentation"** means the user and administrator documentation published
  with the Software (including `README.md` and the `docs/` directory) for the
  relevant Release.
- **"Support Plan"** means the support tier named in the Order Form, as
  described in Schedule A (GWatch Support Plan).
- **"Order Form"** means Schedule B, and any later order form both parties
  sign that refers to this Agreement.
- **"Customer Modifications"** means changes to the Software made by or for
  Customer, other than by JX Holdings.
- **"Deliverables"** means code, configuration, scripts or documents JX
  Holdings creates for Customer under a Statement of Work.
- **"Fees"** means the amounts set out in the Order Form or a Statement of Work.
- **"Affiliate"** means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under
  common control with a party, for as long as that control lasts.

## 3. Support

**3.1 Support Plan.** During the Term, JX Holdings will provide support under
the Support Plan in Schedule A at the tier named in the Order Form.

**3.2 Response targets.** The response times in Schedule A are targets for a
first substantive response. They are not guarantees of a fix, a workaround or a
resolution time. Customer's only remedies for missed targets are the service
credits in Schedule A, if any.

**3.3 Customer's responsibilities.** Customer will:

- (a) name its Authorized Contacts in the Order Form, and keep that list
  current;
- (b) give reasonable information to reproduce and diagnose a problem,
  including the Release in use, platform, database backend, relevant logs and
  steps to reproduce;
- (c) install Releases reasonably promptly once a fix is available; and
- (d) keep its own backups, using GWatch's built-in backup or its own.

**3.4 Fixes go into the product.** Defect fixes are generally delivered in a
public Release so that every GWatch user benefits. JX Holdings may give
Customer an interim patch build first.

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and runs for an

**7.5 Survival.** Sections 5, 6 (for amounts owed), 7.4, 7.5, 8, 9.4, 10, 11,
12 and 14 survive termination.

## 12. Data

GWatch runs on Customer's own infrastructure and does not send monitoring data
to JX Holdings. JX Holdings receives Customer data only when Customer chooses
to share it for support: logs, configuration exports, screenshots or backups,
or during a remote session Customer starts. Customer should remove
credentials and anything it does not need to share before sending. JX
Holdings will use shared data only to provide support, treat it as
Confidential Information, and delete it within `[30]` days after the related
request is closed unless Customer asks it to keep it longer. JX Holdings will
not access Customer systems except in a session Customer initiates and
controls.
